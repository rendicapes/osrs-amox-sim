⛔⛔ AND A CORRECTION THIS MODEL OWES THIS PAGE: THE WALK IS NOT WHAT IS WORKING. I shipped the rectangle as “keeps the autos out of the entrance.” Isolated properly on the Redemption route, that is wrong. Pin only the FARM tile and leave the step-off free: 142/160, identical to no ring at all. Swap the farm tiles for 17-18-19, or for the same tile four times: still 160/160. Pin the step-off to a single square instead: back to 142/160. And the Redemption route spends 97% of its ticks inside tiles 1-9, so it was never walking a rectangle. The entire effect is which entrance square you step off onto, cycle by cycle.
★★ AND THE REAL HEADLINE IS BIGGER THAN THE RING. Across 640 runs and every configuration above, every plan-A death lands at t63 or t97 and not one lands after. Control: 18 deaths, latest t97. A bad rotation: 150 deaths, latest t97. A good one: zero. The Redemption route’s kill rate is not a survival rate, it is the probability you survive the first ninety-seven ticks, and the ring is a way of scripting that opening. Everything after the third icicle is free.
⚠ What the model cannot explain yet: bolt-holes 7,8,9 score 160/160, 7,7,9,9 scores 160/160, 7,8 scores 131, and 7,9 scores 10. It is not “rotate more is better.” Left measured rather than theorised, and the shipped ring is the one verified over four disjoint blocks of forty seeds.
The shipped route is still: farm 10 → 13 → 15 → 12, stepping off onto 7, 7, 9, 9. Two of the four legs are a single running step, because running covers two tiles and skips the middle one, so the middle column (11/14) is never trodden and never iced.
⚠ THE HYBRID CANNOT TAKE A RING and that is not a tuning failure: its speed comes from arming beside whatever patch happens to be live, and pinning it to a plan costs it recoil outright. Run the hybrid ring-free.
★ WHY THE RING WORKS, TRACED RATHER THAN GUESSED. Every death on the Redemption route across 80 seeds finishes on t63 or t97, the second or third icicle. Past that the run never dies. The shape is always the same: the dump leaves FIVE hitpoints (1 + heal − instances), the reservation for the special fires, the walk into tiles 1-9 has to stop on an iced entrance square, and the floor rolls 5. Five is inside the floor’s 3-5 band, so any live patch is lethal there. The entrance ices because the character is standing in it when the autos land, and a corridor ring is exactly the fix, because it keeps the autos one square outside tiles 1-9 and leaves the reservation somewhere clean to stand. The opening is the fight.
★ RING SUPPLY, ME: “I can use unnoted rings and not refill; it will just take like 10 inventory slots, maybe 11.” Modelled and it is the right call. The noted refresh is a two-tick dialogue that is lost outright if anything lands inside it; the swap is one click that cannot be aborted. 40 charges a ring × 10 rings = exactly the 400 hitpoints of the quest variant, and the run never went dry on 10. Ring-handling ticks per run: A 21 → 9, B 38 → 9, H 21 → 7. The price is ~4 recoil a run on the Redemption route, because a ring that crumbles mid-stall takes the rest of that dump with it, about 11 ticks over a 26-minute fight. Given the slip table below, one unabortable click beats two fragile ones every time. Post-quest wants 13 rings, not 10.
The hybrid is 2.5× faster than A and 1.7× faster than B, 11 minutes instead of 27, at some cost in reliability. All three post blockRisk 0. ⚠ But see the slip table below: the hybrid’s speed does not survive contact with a human any better than the Redemption route’s does.
