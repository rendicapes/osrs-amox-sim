ENGINE v16 · marker POISONCOUNTER-V16 · the numbers below are what this build produces
At the stock 23 antelope the plan banks 118 recoil of a 319 consumable ceiling, against her 400. Recoil rises with every extra antelope carried — the plateau that made this build unreadable is gone — and the sim’s modelled kill lands at 115 antelope, 40 of 40 seeds. That is the sim’s number, not a shopping list: it is two points of slack on a deterministic planner, and the two previous answers (+21, then +89) each looked this solid on the day. The honest requirement is a spread, and where it lands is decided entirely by whether the food phase ever gets the expiry rotation.
Alarms all clean: blockRisk 0, spikeLeak 0, lostRecoil 0 across 420 runs. blockRisk is the one counter where non-zero is not a worse score but an invalid run — an ice block is combat experience that cannot be undone.
THE OPEN PROBLEM IS THE CEILING, NOT A BUG. Every point of recoil needs one instance of coverage and instances come only from consumables: food 207 + cure 90 + null 22 = 319. That is 81 short of 400 at perfect play, so the fight is not winnable on the current kit no matter how well it is executed. Dodging, tick-saving and stall-fitting improve survival, not coverage. The lever is a fourth coverage source or more antelope — nothing in the routing.
What v16 changed: the poison counter is 100, not 19, and a cure spends five counter points where a poison tick spends one. The old model charged both a single point, so every poison tick burned a whole cure. 114 → 118 at stock and the kill threshold fell from 120 antelope to 115. ⚠ The catch: a leaked poison tick now hits for 20, not 4 — unstalled at 10 hitpoints that is instant death, which is why poisonAnchor matters more than it used to.
Two parameter questions closed by Rendi this session, both were live in the file: restDoses stays 3 — the rests are made in the fight out of the looting bag, and a freshly-made Guthix rest is 3-dose; the 4-dose figure in osrs-bug-principles.md describes decanted or GE-bought rests, so both notes were right about different rests. poisonAnchor stays 7; the safe window is {7,8,9} and 7 and 8 score identically at every food count.
